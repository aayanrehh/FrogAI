# FrogAI — 48-Hour Client-Closing Playbook

**Goal:** Close the first paying client (Beta Founder) within 48 hours.
**North star:** Distribution beats product. Ship the outcome, not the tool.

This plan fuses the `FrogAI_CoFounder_Strategy.md` roadmap with Greg Isenberg's agent-SaaS playbook:
- **Distribution is the moat, not the product.** Outbound + proof start day one.
- **Productize the service.** One clean packaged offer now; a client login dashboard later (retention + integration).
- **Land-and-expand (Trojan horse).** Free Leap reel → meeting → sell Ribbit (the real pain: speed-to-lead).
- **Pain-point scraping / digital homesteading.** Go where agents publicly express pain (reviews, Reddit, X) and *help*, don't pitch.
- **Ship the outcome.** Sell booked tours + cinematic video, not "an AI agent."
- **Speed-to-lead is the product AND the pitch.** We reply in <3s; prove it by following up with our own leads fast.
- **Niche down to win.** Dominate CT luxury / Top-50 agents first, then brokerages.

---

## Phase 0 — Lock the offer (now, ~2h)
1. Keep the stripped landing page (done) + Calendly wired (done).
2. Collapse Tadpole/Bullfrog into **ONE Beta Founder offer** so outreach has a single ask:
   - "FrogAI Beta Founder — $199/mo, no setup fee. Includes 4 Leap cinematic reels/mo + always-on Ribbit speed-to-lead. Month-to-month, cancel anytime." (Pricing from strategy §5; discount is the close incentive.)
3. Confirm Higgsfield + a temporary Ribbit (even a manual/semi-auto SMS reply is fine for the first demo) are ready to fulfill the promise.

## Phase 1 — Generate the bait (T-48h → T-36h, ~4h)
4. Use the **distribution agent** to pull CT luxury listings (> $1M, on market > 30 days, no video tour) from Zillow / Realtor.com.
5. Run 5 of them through Higgsfield → produce 15s Leap teaser reels. These are the value-first gift.

## Phase 2 — Build the lead queue (T-36h, runs via cron)
6. Launch the distribution agent scan across Realtor.com, Zillow, Reddit, X. Target: **50 qualified pain signals in 24h.**
   - Signals: "slow to respond" / "hard to reach" reviews, listings with no video, stale luxury inventory, agents complaining about lead gen, high-volume low-tech teams.
7. Agent ranks + drafts a value-first message per signal (Trojan horse: free reel + Ribbit hook). Human approves/sends (no auto-spam).

## Phase 3 — Outreach (T-24h → T-12h)
8. Send value-first DMs/emails to the top 20-30 signals:
   > "Noticed your listing at [Address]. I ran your photos through our AI video engine — here's a free cinematic reel for your Instagram. We also reply to Zillow leads in under 3 seconds. Open to a 10-min chat?"
9. Practice what we preach: any reply gets a human response in **< 3 minutes** (dogfood Ribbit).

## Phase 4 — Follow-up + close (T-12h → T-0)
10. Second touch to opens/no-replies (Ribbit-style 2-step sequence).
11. Book 3-5 strategy calls via the Calendly link on the page.
12. Demo: show the free reel + a live <3s lead reply. Close on the Beta Founder offer with a one-page agreement.

---

## Structural next steps (after first client)
- **Build the client dashboard/portal** (the productization step you flagged): a login where clients see their Leap reels, Ribbit reply logs, and booked tours. This turns a service into a retentive SaaS.
- **Finish the agents:** Ribbit on LangGraph (stateful, CRM-connected); Leap on Higgsfield (production pipeline).
- **Brokerage partnerships:** after 3 CT case studies, pitch William Raveis / Coldwell Banker as preferred vendor (5-10 seats/deal).
- **Scale math:** ~17 Bullfrog clients = $10K MRR (strategy §5).
